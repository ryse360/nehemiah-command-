# Nehemiah Living Organism Design

## Goal

Replace the decorative CSS circle with a procedural, state-driven intelligence artifact while preserving the existing Founder journey.

## Preserved states

- resting
- listening
- focus-surfaced
- decision-required
- action-underway
- proof-created

## Requirements

- The organism is always subtly alive.
- No words appear inside the organism.
- Motion represents actual application state.
- The environment remains ivory rather than dark.
- Warm gold represents significance and proof.
- Reduced-motion and WebGL fallbacks are required.
- Initial development remains isolated at `/lab/organism`.
- Authentication, AI orchestration, memory and decision workflows remain untouched until approval.

## State behavior

- **Resting:** quiet core, slow circulation, low motion.
- **Listening:** energy contracts inward and becomes attentive.
- **Focus surfaced:** movement becomes coherent and directional.
- **Decision required:** one pathway dominates while surrounding motion quiets.
- **Action underway:** energy moves outward toward execution.
- **Proof created:** strongest controlled gold emphasis and resolved illumination.
