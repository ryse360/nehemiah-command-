# Founder Pilot Protocol

The v1.0 release gate requires at least five real Founder sessions.

For each session record:
- The consequential command
- Whether the complete decision journey was completed
- Whether visible proof was captured
- Whether the prepared decision was useful
- Whether the Founder could answer within five seconds: what matters, why first, what requires Founder authority, what action follows, and what counts as proof
- Critical and high-severity issues, including the count of high issues explicitly accepted by the Founder
- Bounded notes

Release remains blocked until five sessions pass, all critical issues are resolved, all high issues are resolved or explicitly accepted, and the Founder provides explicit release approval.
