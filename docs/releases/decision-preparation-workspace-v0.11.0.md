# Decision Preparation Workspace v0.11.0

## Purpose

Convert readiness gaps into an active preparation workflow instead of stopping at diagnosis.

## Delivered

- One actionable preparation item for every missing readiness dimension.
- Dimension-specific prompts, required outputs, and suggested actions for evidence, ownership, capacity, boundaries, and proof.
- Validation that prevents vague or empty responses from being treated as resolution.
- Founder-supplied resolution records preserved inside the live preparation workspace.
- Real-time readiness recalculation after each resolved item.
- Automatic decision-gate progression from blocked to conditional or open when requirements are completed.
- Dedicated preparation workspace integrated into the decision chamber.

## Governance

Nehemiah may organize and validate preparation, but it cannot invent the missing operating detail. A readiness gap is only resolved after a specific Founder-supplied response is recorded. The Founder remains the decision authority.
