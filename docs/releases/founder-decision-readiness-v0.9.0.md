# Founder Decision Readiness — v0.9.0

## Purpose

Prevent underprepared issues from being placed before the Founder as if they were decision-ready.

## Readiness dimensions

Nehemiah evaluates five conditions:

1. **Evidence** — the decision is grounded in a defined operating signal or preserved precedent.
2. **Ownership** — an accountable owner or operating function is explicit.
3. **Capacity** — the resource cost and displaced priority are visible.
4. **Boundaries** — scope, limits, and stopping conditions protect the decision.
5. **Proof** — visible evidence is defined before expansion or completion.

## Status rules

- **Ready:** 5 of 5 dimensions complete.
- **Conditional:** 3–4 dimensions complete; unresolved conditions are shown explicitly.
- **Not ready:** fewer than 3 dimensions complete; Nehemiah should continue preparation before escalation.

## High-risk precedent rule

When strategic recall identifies a costly precedent, generic confidence is insufficient. Readiness requires preserved proof or a lesson from the prior decision; otherwise evidence remains unresolved.

## Interface behavior

The readiness card appears first in the decision chamber so the Founder sees whether the issue is prepared before reviewing precedent, consequence mapping, and disposition controls.

## Governance

- Readiness prepares judgment; it does not make the decision.
- A conditional decision may be surfaced only with unresolved conditions visible.
- A not-ready issue should remain in preparation rather than being framed as a Founder decision.
- The Founder remains final authority.
