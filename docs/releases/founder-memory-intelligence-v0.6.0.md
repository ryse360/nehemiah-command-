# Founder Memory Intelligence — v0.6.0

## Purpose

Turn completed Founder decisions into reusable strategic intelligence rather than passive history.

## Delivered

- Search across commands, decision notes, visible actions, proof, and lessons.
- Filter history by Founder disposition.
- Preserve an explicit lesson during proof capture.
- Detect recurring decision patterns.
- Surface prior decisions resembling the current Founder command.
- Summarize completed decisions, recurring patterns, and preserved lessons.
- Keep all intelligence local to the Founder browser in this release.

## Governance

- Similarity results are decision precedents, not automatic recommendations.
- The Founder remains the decision authority.
- Patterns require at least two supporting decisions.
- Inferred lessons are labeled through deterministic rules and never presented as external facts.
- No cloud synchronization or third-party transfer is enabled.
