# Founder Strategic Recall — v0.7.0

## Purpose

Bring relevant Founder precedent into the live decision chamber before a consequential decision is made.

## Delivered

- Selects the strongest materially related prior decision for the active command.
- Explains shared terms and material differences between the current decision and its precedent.
- Surfaces the precedent's preserved lesson and proof.
- Warns when the closest precedent contains evidence of failure, delay, overrun, stalled execution, or insufficient proof.
- Poses a Founder-owned strategic question before disposition.
- Avoids interrupting the decision chamber when similarity is too weak.

## Governance

- Recall is precedent, not an automatic recommendation.
- The Founder remains the final decision authority.
- Warnings are grounded only in preserved Founder records.
- Nehemiah does not manufacture similarity from a single generic term.
- Strategic recall is visible only when a decision reaches the decision-required state.
- Memory remains local to the current Founder browser in this release.
