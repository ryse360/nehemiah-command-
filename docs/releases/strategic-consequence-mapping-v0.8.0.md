# Strategic Consequence Mapping v0.8.0

## Purpose

Operationalize the Founder laws: every move must serve a purpose, and every first move must anticipate the likely response and prepare the continuation.

## Delivered

- Three-move consequence map inside the live decision chamber.
- Likely immediate response.
- Second-order consequence.
- Resource tradeoff.
- Prepared continuation.
- Explicit stop condition.
- Assumptions available on demand.
- Higher-risk continuation when Founder memory contains costly precedent.

## Governance

- Consequence maps prepare judgment; they do not choose for the Founder.
- High-risk classification must be grounded in preserved proof or lessons.
- Every continuation includes a boundary or stop condition.
- The map appears only when Founder authority is required.
